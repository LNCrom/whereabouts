# Whereabouts Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Whereabouts is a personal family location dashboard built around consent and member-controlled sharing.

## Data Collection and Use

Whereabouts uses consent-based location sharing. When you create or accept a Whereabouts invite and enable location sharing, the app may store the following information in Apple's iCloud/CloudKit service so invited family members can view it in the app:

- Your device display name
- Your current latitude and longitude
- A best-effort street address or coordinate fallback for the current location
- An estimated arrival time based on observed location fixes
- The time your location was last updated
- Your private iCloud user record identifier, used by CloudKit to keep one current location record per approved participant

Whereabouts does not sell personal data and does not use location data for advertising.

Contact selection, when used, helps you choose people to invite. Whereabouts does not upload your address book to the developer.

## Location Data

Whereabouts is designed for consent-based family location sharing. The app does not provide hidden tracking and does not access private Apple Find My location data. Apple does not make Find My people-location data available to third-party apps.

Sharing requires the person sharing their location to install/open Whereabouts, accept an Apple iCloud share invite, and grant iOS location permission. You can pause sharing in the app or revoke location permission in iOS Settings.

New installations require you to enable sharing. Approximate sharing rounds coordinates before upload and omits the street address. The app retains the latest unsent location, arrival estimate, and pending removal actions in a protected local file excluded from backups. It retries temporary failures when iOS allows the app to run. A pending location older than five minutes is not uploaded as a new fix.

Pausing or signing out stops new location uploads and requests removal of your current cloud location. Removal cannot complete while your phone is offline. Other members may see the last known location until the removal reaches iCloud. The map labels old fixes as last known and does not claim they are live.

Invitations are restricted to selected Apple accounts. Members with read/write access to the shared CloudKit zone are trusted collaborators; CloudKit zone sharing does not enforce ownership of individual location records within that zone. The owner can manage participants and revoke access in Apple's sharing sheet. Leaving a circle removes that account's access; the owner deleting a circle removes the shared data and access for everyone.

## Children and Families

Whereabouts is intended for personal family coordination. Parents or guardians should manage consent for children in their family group.
